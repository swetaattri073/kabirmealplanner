Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 September 2026 · Applies to littlebowl.in and the LittleBowl Android / iOS apps

LittleBowl (“we”, “us”, “our”) helps parents plan and log meals for toddlers. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using LittleBowl you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

LittleBowl is operated at https://littlebowl.in. For privacy questions or data requests, contact us at littlebowlmealplanner@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use LittleBowl, we may collect:

  • Account information — name (optional), email address, and a hashed password when you register.
  • Child / toddler profiles — name, age or birth date, gender, weight/height if you enter them, allergies, diet preference, health notes, feeding preferences (for example school lunchbox or hidden-veggie preferences), and meal schedule.
  • Meal & nutrition data — foods logged, portions, reactions, notes, weekly meal plans, growth records you add, and saved recipes.
  • Photos — meal photos you choose to attach when logging a meal. Photos are stored on our servers only when you save a log that includes them.
  • Guest / device identifiers — an anonymous guest ID (cookie and/or on-device storage) so you can use LittleBowl without an account; on mobile, a device ID may be used for chat usage limits.
  • Chat messages — if you use the in-app assistant, messages you send and related meal-plan context may be processed to generate a reply. We store usage counts; full chat transcripts are not retained as a permanent server archive.
  • Usage & technical data — pages viewed, approximate session activity, referrer, and IP address for security, debugging, and first-party product analytics. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.
  • Device permissions (mobile) — camera and photo library (only when you take or pick a meal photo); notifications (only if you enable local meal reminders).

3. How we use information

  • Provide meal plans, nutrition insights, recipes, and meal logging.
  • Remember your session (signed-in or guest) across visits.
  • Send or show meal reminders you configure on your device (local notifications).
  • Improve reliability and understand product usage (first-party analytics).
  • Secure the service, prevent abuse, and comply with law.
  • Power the optional AI chat assistant when that feature is enabled for your account.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Legal bases (where applicable)

Where GDPR or similar laws apply, we process data to perform our contract with you (providing the service), with your consent (for example camera access or optional chat), and for legitimate interests such as security and improving LittleBowl.

5. How we share information

We share data only as needed to run LittleBowl:

  • Hosting / infrastructure — data is stored on our application servers and database (for example cloud VMs we operate).
  • OpenAI — if the chat assistant is enabled, message content and limited toddler context (such as name, age, and today’s plan) are sent to OpenAI to generate replies.
  • Food databases — when you add or enrich a custom food, we may query public food databases (for example Open Food Facts or USDA) using the food name or ID you provide — not your child’s personal profile.
  • Legal — if required by law or to protect rights and safety.

We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

6. Cookies and on-device storage

  • Website: session and remember-me cookies, plus a guest ID cookie so anonymous profiles persist. Browser local/session storage may keep guest ID, last toddler, notify preferences, and short-lived chat UI state.
  • Mobile apps: a secure store may hold your login token; other preferences and guest ID may be stored in app storage on your device.

You can clear cookies or uninstall the app to remove most on-device data. Server-side profile data remains until you delete it or request deletion.

7. Children’s privacy

LittleBowl is designed for parents and caregivers, not for children to use on their own. Toddler information is entered by an adult account or guest session you control. We do not knowingly allow children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country) to create their own accounts. If you believe a child provided us data directly, contact littlebowlmealplanner@gmail.com and we will delete it.

8. Data retention

  • Account, toddler profiles, meal logs, plans, and photos are kept while your account or guest profile exists, or until you delete them.
  • Guest identifiers and related cookies are typically kept for up to about 400 days of inactivity on the client side; server data remains until deleted.
  • Analytics and audit records may be kept for operational periods (commonly weeks to months) for security and product insight.
  • Chat usage counters may be kept to enforce fair-use limits.

9. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may:

  • Access or update profile information in the app or website.
  • Delete a toddler profile (which removes associated meal data cascaded with that profile).
  • Request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data by emailing littlebowlmealplanner@gmail.com.
  • Refuse camera, photos, or notifications; core meal planning still works without them.
  • Stop using guest mode by clearing site data / reinstalling, or by creating an account and continuing there.

10. Security

We use industry-standard measures such as HTTPS, hashed passwords, and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; please use a strong unique password.

11. International users

LittleBowl may be hosted in India or other regions where our servers operate. If you use the service from another country, your information may be processed in the country where our servers are located.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Effective date” at the top will change when we do. Continued use of LittleBowl after an update means you accept the revised policy.

13. Contact

Privacy requests: littlebowlmealplanner@gmail.com
Website: https://littlebowl.in
